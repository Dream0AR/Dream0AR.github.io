回顾

横截相交与杯积由Poincaré对偶联系, $PD([S\cap T])=PD([S])\cup PD([T]).$ 一个应用是考虑$H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[\tau]/\tau^{n+1},$ 那么$\tau=PD[H],$ $H$为$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n$中的超平面$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}\hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n.$

另一个应用是Bezout定理. 设$F_i(x,y,z)$为一个齐次多项式, $\deg=d_i,$ $i=1,2.$ 那么$V_i:=\{[x:y:z]|F_i=0\}\subset \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2$称为$\deg d_i$的代数曲线. $V_i$为$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2$中的$1$维子流形.

$[V_1]\in H_2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2)\cong \mathbb{Z}.$ 令$\tau_1:=PD[V_1]\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2)=\mathbb{Z}\{\tau\}.$ 断言$\tau_1=d_1\tau.$ 取$H\subset \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2,$ $H\cap V_1=d_i$个点. 那么$PD(H)\cup PD(V_1)=PD(d_i[\ast ]),$ $\tau\cup \tau_1=d_1\tau^2.$ 这就推知了$\tau_1=d_1\tau.$

从而$\tau_1\cup \tau_2=d_1d_2\tau^2,$ $[V_1\cap V_2]=d_1d_2$个点, 若$V_1,V_2$横截相交.

复向量丛

考虑$\pi:E\rightarrow B,$ 纤维为$\mathbb{C}$-线性空间. 如我们有$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n$上的典范复线丛$\gamma_n^1.$

若$\omega$为$n$维复向量丛, 那么它也是一个$2n$维实向量丛. 此时它总是可定向的实向量丛. 对于向量空间, $\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{R}(\mathbb{R}^{2n},V_\mathbb{R})\cong GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R}),$ $\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{C}^n,V)\cong GL_n(\mathbb{C}).$ 那么$\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{C}^n,V)\subset \operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{R}(\mathbb{R}^{2n},V_\mathbb{R})$诱导了$GL_n(\mathbb{C})\subset GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R}).$ 这就迫使包含只落在$GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R})$的某一个连通分支中, 给出了一个定向.

定义$(\gamma_1^1)^V:=\operatorname{Hom}_\mathbb{C}(\gamma_1^1,\varepsilon),$ 此时$\varepsilon$为平凡复线丛. 这是一个$\dim_\mathbb{C}=1$的$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1$上线丛. 断言$e((\gamma_1^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=\tau=PD([\ast ])\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1;\mathbb{Z}),$ 或者说$\left<{}e,[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1]\right>=1.$

固定一个向量$v\in \mathbb{C}^2\setminus\{0\},$ 我们有$(\gamma_1^1)^V$的截面$L\mapsto (u\mapsto \left<{}u,v\right>).$ 此时

$$ Z_s=\{L:s(L)=0\}=\{\left<{}v\right>^\perp\}, $$

为$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1$中的一个点. 这样就有

$$ e((\gamma_1^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=PD([Z_s])=PD([\ast ])=\tau. $$

一般地, $e((\gamma_n^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z}).$

Chern类

设$\omega$为复向量丛$\mathbb{C}^n\rightarrow E\rightarrow B,$ $B$为CW复形. 取投影丛

$$ \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}\xrightarrow{\iota} P(E)\rightarrow B. $$

回忆由Leray-Hirsch, $H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1};\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[b]/b^n.$ 事实上选取$b=-\tau,$ 因为$b=e((\tau^1_{n-1})_\mathbb{R}).$

那么$H^\ast (P(E))=H^\ast (B)\{1,x,\cdots,x^{n-1}\},$ $H^\ast (P(E))\xrightarrow{\iota^\ast }H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}),$ $x\mapsto b.$ 定义Chern类为$c_i=c_i(\omega)\in H^{2i}(B;\mathbb{Z}),$ 使得$H^\ast (P(E);\mathbb{Z})$中有$x^n-c_1x^{n-1}+c_2x^{n-2}-\cdots (-1)^nc_n=0.$

Chern类满足如下性质:

  1. $c_0=1,$ $c_i=0,$ $\,\forall\,i>n=\dim_\mathbb{C}(\omega);$

  2. $c_i(f^\ast \omega)=f^\ast c_i(\omega);$

  3. $c(\omega\oplus\eta)=c(\omega)\cup c(\eta),$ $c(\omega):=\sum_{i=0}^\infty c_i(\omega);$

  4. $c_1(\gamma_1^1)=b=-\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1;\mathbb{Z}),$ $\left<{}c_1(\gamma_1^1),[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1]\right>=-1.$

为什么选取$b=-\tau$? 我们选取有$c_1(\gamma_n^1)=e((\gamma_n^1)_\mathbb{R})=-\tau.$ 我们希望$c_1=e,$ 这就不得不选取相反的符号了. 不过这样的好处是我们有$c_n(\omega)=e(\omega_\mathbb{R}),$ $\,\forall\,$秩$n$复向量丛$\omega.$

我们有分裂原理, 此时是系数为$\mathbb{Z}$的复版本. 可以用它来给出证明.

有这些性质, 我们进而有如下结论:

  1. $c(\varepsilon^n)=1,$

  2. $c_i(\omega\oplus \varepsilon^n)=c_i(\omega),$

  3. $\omega^n$有$k$个独立截面, $\omega^n=\varepsilon^k\oplus\eta^{n-k},$ $c_i(\omega^n)=c_i(\eta^{n-k})=0,$ $\,\forall\,i>n-k.$

  4. 每个仿紧底空间上的$\omega$都能配备Hermitian度量.

  5. 若$\omega\cong \eta,$ 则$c_i(\omega)=c_i(\eta).$

  6. $c_1(\gamma_n^1)=b=-\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z}).$

复Grassmannian流形

$G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}):=\{X|X\subset \mathbb{C}^{n+k},\dim_\mathbb{C}X=n\}.$ 类似地, 它是一个紧光滑流形, 为$U_{n+k}/U_n\times U_k.$ 实维数为$2nk.$ 我们有$G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})$上的典范丛$\gamma^n.$

定理 1. 每个仿紧空间上的秩$n$复向量丛都有一个到$G_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)$上的$\gamma^n$的丛映照.

定理 2. $H^\ast (G_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty);\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[c_1,\cdots,c_n].$

可以类似实版本证明, 另一种方式为谱序列. 定义$V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}):=\{(v_1,\cdots,v_n)|v_i\in \mathbb{C}^{n+k}, \perp\}.$ 我们有一个纤维丛:

$$ U_n\rightarrow V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})\rightarrow G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}), $$

更清楚地来看:

$$ U_n\rightarrow U_{n+k}/U_k\rightarrow U_{n+k}/U_n\times U_k $$

可以验证$V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})$是$2k$-连通的, $U_k\hookrightarrow U_{n+k}$诱导了$\pi_{\le 2k}$的同构. 因此$V_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)\cong \ast ,$ 存在纤维化 $$ U_n\rightarrow V_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)\rightarrow G_n(C^\infty). $$

那么由LSSS, $E_2^{p,q}=H^p(G_n)\otimes H^q(U_n)\Rightarrow H^\ast (\ast ).$ 回忆$H^\ast (U_n)=\Lambda^\ast (x_1,x_3,\cdots,x_{2n-1}).$ 分析谱序列可得$H^\ast (G_n)=\mathbb{Z}[c_1,\cdots,c_n].$

review

Transverse intersection and cup product are connected by Poincaré duality, $PD([S\cap T])=PD([S])\cup PD([T]).$ One application is to consider $H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[\tau]/\tau^{n+1},$ Then $\tau=PD[H],$ $H$ is the hyperplane $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}\hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n.$ in $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n$

Another application is Bezout's theorem. Let $F_i(x,y,z)$ be a homogeneous polynomial, $\deg=d_i,$ $i=1,2.$ Then $V_i:=\{[x:y:z]|F_i=0\}\subset \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2$ is called the algebraic curve of $\deg d_i$. $V_i$ is the $1$-dimensional submanifold in $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2$.

$[V_1]\in H_2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2)\cong \mathbb{Z}.$ Order$\tau_1:=PD[V_1]\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2)=\mathbb{Z}\{\tau\}.$ Assert$\tau_1=d_1\tau.$ Take$H\subset \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^2,$ $H\cap V_1=d_i$ points. Then $PD(H)\cup PD(V_1)=PD(d_i[\ast ]),$ $\tau\cup \tau_1=d_1\tau^2.$ This infers $\tau_1=d_1\tau.$

Thus $\tau_1\cup \tau_2=d_1d_2\tau^2,$ $[V_1\cap V_2]=d_1d_2$ points, If $V_1,V_2$ intersects transversely.

complex vector bundle

Consider the $\pi:E\rightarrow B,$ fiber as a $\mathbb{C}$-linear space. For example, we have the canonical complex complex $\gamma_n^1.$ on $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n$

If $\omega$ is a $n$-dimensional complex vector bundle, then it is also a $2n$-dimensional real vector bundle. At this time it is always an orientable real vector bundle. For vector spaces, $\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{R}(\mathbb{R}^{2n},V_\mathbb{R})\cong GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R}),$ $\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{C}^n,V)\cong GL_n(\mathbb{C}).$ Then $\operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{C}(\mathbb{C}^n,V)\subset \operatorname{Iso}_\mathbb{R}(\mathbb{R}^{2n},V_\mathbb{R})$ induced $GL_n(\mathbb{C})\subset GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R}).$ This forces the inclusion to fall only in a certain connected component of $GL_{2n}(\mathbb{R})$, An orientation is given.

Definition$(\gamma_1^1)^V:=\operatorname{Hom}_\mathbb{C}(\gamma_1^1,\varepsilon),$ At this time $\varepsilon$ is a trivial polylinear bundle. This is an $\dim_\mathbb{C}=1$ upper line bundle of $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1$. Assertion$e((\gamma_1^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=\tau=PD([\ast ])\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1;\mathbb{Z}),$ Or $\left<{}e,[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1]\right>=1.$

Fix a vector $v\in \mathbb{C}^2\setminus\{0\},$ We have section $L\mapsto (u\mapsto \left<{}u,v\right>).$ of $(\gamma_1^1)^V$ At this time

$$ Z_s=\{L:s(L)=0\}=\{\left<{}v\right>^\perp\}, $$

is a point in $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1$. In this way, we have

$$ e((\gamma_1^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=PD([Z_s])=PD([\ast ])=\tau. $$

Generally, $e((\gamma_n^1)^V_\mathbb{R})=\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z}).$

Chern class

Let $\omega$ be the complex vector bundle $\mathbb{C}^n\rightarrow E\rightarrow B,$ $B$ is the CW complex. Take the projection bundle

$$ \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}\xrightarrow{\iota} P(E)\rightarrow B. $$

Memories by Leray-Hirsch, $H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1};\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[b]/b^n.$ Actually select $b=-\tau,$ because $b=e((\tau^1_{n-1})_\mathbb{R}).$

Then $H^\ast (P(E))=H^\ast (B)\{1,x,\cdots,x^{n-1}\},$ $H^\ast (P(E))\xrightarrow{\iota^\ast }H^\ast (\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{n-1}),$ $x\mapsto b.$ Define the Chern class as $c_i=c_i(\omega)\in H^{2i}(B;\mathbb{Z}),$ Make $H^\ast (P(E);\mathbb{Z})$ contain $x^n-c_1x^{n-1}+c_2x^{n-2}-\cdots (-1)^nc_n=0.$

The Chern class satisfies the following properties:

  1. $c_0=1,$ $c_i=0,$ $\,\forall\,i>n=\dim_\mathbb{C}(\omega);$

  2. $c_i(f^\ast \omega)=f^\ast c_i(\omega);$

  3. $c(\omega\oplus\eta)=c(\omega)\cup c(\eta),$ $c(\omega):=\sum_{i=0}^\infty c_i(\omega);$

  4. $c_1(\gamma_1^1)=b=-\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1;\mathbb{Z}),$ $\left<{}c_1(\gamma_1^1),[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1]\right>=-1.$

Why choose $b=-\tau$? We select $c_1(\gamma_n^1)=e((\gamma_n^1)_\mathbb{R})=-\tau.$ We want $c_1=e,$ to have to choose the opposite sign. But the advantage of this is that we have $c_n(\omega)=e(\omega_\mathbb{R}),$ $\,\forall\,$Rank$n$Complex vector bundle$\omega.$

We have the splitting principle, which is now a complex version with coefficient $\mathbb{Z}$. We can use it to give a proof.

With these properties, we have the following conclusions:

  1. $c(\varepsilon^n)=1,$

  2. $c_i(\omega\oplus \varepsilon^n)=c_i(\omega),$

  3. $\omega^n$ has $k$ independent sections, $\omega^n=\varepsilon^k\oplus\eta^{n-k},$ $c_i(\omega^n)=c_i(\eta^{n-k})=0,$ $\,\forall\,i>n-k.$

  4. Every $\omega$ on the paracompact base space can be equipped with a Hermitian metric.

  5. If $\omega\cong \eta,$ then $c_i(\omega)=c_i(\eta).$

  6. $c_1(\gamma_n^1)=b=-\tau\in H^2(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^n;\mathbb{Z}).$

Complex Grassmannian manifold

$G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}):=\{X|X\subset \mathbb{C}^{n+k},\dim_\mathbb{C}X=n\}.$ Similarly, it is a compact smooth manifold with $U_{n+k}/U_n\times U_k.$ and real dimension $2nk.$ We have the canonical bundle $\gamma^n.$ on $G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})$

Theorem 1 . Every complex vector bundle of rank $n$ on a paracompact space has a bundle mapping to $\gamma^n$ on $G_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)$.

Theorem 2 . $H^\ast (G_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty);\mathbb{Z})=\mathbb{Z}[c_1,\cdots,c_n].$

The proof can be similar to the real version, and another way is spectral sequence. Definition$V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}):=\{(v_1,\cdots,v_n)|v_i\in \mathbb{C}^{n+k}, \perp\}.$ We have a fiber bundle:

$$ U_n\rightarrow V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})\rightarrow G_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k}), $$

To see it more clearly:

$$ U_n\rightarrow U_{n+k}/U_k\rightarrow U_{n+k}/U_n\times U_k $$

It can be verified that $V_n(\mathbb{C}^{n+k})$ is $2k$-connected, $U_k\hookrightarrow U_{n+k}$ induces the isomorphism of $\pi_{\le 2k}$. Therefore $V_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)\cong \ast ,$ fibrosis is present $$ U_n\rightarrow V_n(\mathbb{C}^\infty)\rightarrow G_n(C^\infty). $$

Then by LSSS, $E_2^{p,q}=H^p(G_n)\otimes H^q(U_n)\Rightarrow H^\ast (\ast ).$ Memories$H^\ast (U_n)=\Lambda^\ast (x_1,x_3,\cdots,x_{2n-1}).$ Analyzing the spectrum sequence can obtain $H^\ast (G_n)=\mathbb{Z}[c_1,\cdots,c_n].$
