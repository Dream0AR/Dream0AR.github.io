Pontrjagin数

对于实流形$M^{4n},$ 为一个定向闭流形, $I=(i_1,\cdots,i_r)$为$n$的划分. 定义第$I$个Pontrjagin数为

$$ P_I[M^{4n}]=\left<{}p_{i_1}\cdots p_{i_r},[M^{4n}]\right>\in \mathbb{Z}. $$

注 1. 记$\overline M$为$M$反定向, $[\overline M]=-[M],$ 但$p_k(\tau_M)=p_k(\tau_{\overline M}),$ 从而$P_I[\overline M]=-p_I[M].$

推论 2. 若$M^{4n}$ Pontrjagin数非零, 那么不存在反定向的微分同胚$M\rightarrow M.$

若$M=\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m,$ $p(\tau_{\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m})=(1+a^2)^{m+1},$ $a=-c_1(\gamma^1),$ $p_k(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m)=\binom{m+1}{k} a^{2k}\in H^{4k}.$ 若$m=2n,$ 则$M^{4n}=\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n},$ $\,\forall\,I$为$n$的划分, $p_{i_1}\cdots p_{i_r}[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}]=\binom{2n+1}{i_1}\cdots \binom{2n+1}{i_r}\neq 0.$ 作为一个推论, $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}$上不存在反定向的微分自同胚.

对于$m$为奇数的情形, 复共轭是一个反定向的微分自同胚. 注意$m$为奇数时, 没有top Pontrjagin类.

命题 3. 若$M^{4n}=\partial V^{4n+1},$ $V$紧定向, 则$M^{4n}$所有的Pontrjagin数为零.

推论 4. $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}\neq \partial V^{4n+1}$(定向), 且有限copy $\sqcup \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}\neq \partial V.$

注意对$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n+1}$不对, 有例子$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1\approx S^2\approx \partial D^3.$

对称函数论

回顾$T^n=U(1)^{\times n}\hookrightarrow U(n),$ 映到对角阵中. 这诱导了$BT^n\rightarrow BU(n),$ 拉回得到$H^\ast (BT^n;\mathbb{Z})\leftarrow H^\ast (BU(n);\mathbb{Z}),$ $c_i\mapsto \sigma_i(t_1,\cdots t_n).$ $1+\sigma_1+\cdots+\sigma_n=(1+t_1)\cdots (1+t_n).$

记$S=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]=\oplus_{k=0}^\infty S^k,$ $S^k$为$k$次对称多项式. 它的基为

$$ \{\sigma_{i_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_r}|i_1+\cdots+i_r=k,\quad i_j\le n\}. $$

$$ S^k=\mathbb{Z}\{\sigma_I|I\text{ 为$k$的划分, 每个元小于等于$n$}\}. $$

称两个单项等价, 若它们差一个置换. 如$t_1t_2^3t_3^5\sim t_4t_7^3t_2^5.$ 对$k$划分$I,$ $I$单项对称多项式定义为

$$ m_I:=\sum \text{ 单项等价于 }t^I:=t_1^{i_1}\cdots t_r^{i_r}. $$

例如$k=3,$ $I=2+1=3,$ $m_I=t_1t_2^2+t_1^2t_2+t_1t_3^2+\cdots.$

引理 5. $\{m_I|\text{$I$为$k$划分, $r\le n$.}\}$构成了$S^k=S^k_{(n)}$的一组$\mathbb{Z}$-基.

这组元素显然张成了$S^k.$ 只需检验维数.

$$ \dim_\mathbb{Z}S^k_{(n)}=\#\{\sigma_I\}=\{I|i_j\le n\}. $$

希望证明它和$\{I|r\le n\}$相等. (都是$k$划分.)

我们发现前者为$n$列以下的Young diagrams with $k$ boxes, 后者为$n$行以下的Young diagrams with $k$ boxes. 例如对于$I=(4,4,3,1,1),$ 逆序排列, 像俄罗斯方块一样放置, 那么要求列数不超过$n$. 和它对应的是$I'=(5,3,3,2),$ 同样逆序排列按俄罗斯方块放置, 要求行数不超过$n.$ 它们之间由转置联系. 因此集合数相同.

对于任意$k$划分$I,$

$$ m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_n)\in \mathbb{Z}[t_1,\cdots,t_n]^{S_n}=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]. $$

$$ m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_n)=S_I(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n). $$

可以验证, $\,\forall\,n<k,$

$$ S_{I,n}(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n)=S_{I,k}(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n,0,\cdots,0); $$

$\,\forall\,n\ge k,$ $S_I$不变.

因此, 若$n\ge k,$ 那么$\{m_I\}=\{S_I(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n)\}$构成了$S^k=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]$的一组$\mathbb{Z}$-基.

例如, $k=1,$ $S_1(\sigma_1)=\sigma_1,$

$$ S_2(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)=\sigma_1^2-2\sigma_2=(t_1+t_2)^2-2t_1t_2=t_1^2+t_2^2=m_2(t_1,t_2). $$

$$ S_{1,1}(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)=t_1t_2=\sigma_2, $$

$$ S _3=\sigma_1^3-3\sigma_1\sigma_2+3\sigma_3. $$

应用到拓扑上,

$$ H^\ast (BU(n))\rightarrow H^\ast (BU(1)^{\times n})^{S_n},\quad c_i\mapsto \sigma_i(t_1,\cdots,t_n), $$

$\{c_I|I\text{ 为$k$的划分, }i_j\le n\}$构成了$H^{2k}(BU(n))$的一组$\mathbb{Z}$基. 改变基后, $\{S_I(c_1,\cdots,c_n)|r\le n\}$也是一组$\mathbb{Z}$基.

对秩$n$复向量丛$\omega,$ 定义$S_I(c(\omega)):=S_I(c_1(\omega),\cdots,c_n(\omega))\in H^{2n}(B;\mathbb{Z}).$

引理 6 (Thom). $S_I(c(\omega\oplus\omega'))=\sum_{JK=I}S_J(c(\omega))S_K(c(\omega')).$

考虑$\sigma_k$为$k$基本对称多项式($t_1,\cdots,t_n$), $\sigma_k'$为$(t_{n+1},\cdots,t_{n+m}),$ $\sigma_k''=\sum_{i=0}^k\sigma_i\sigma_{k-i}',$ 验证有$\sigma_k''$为$(t_1,\cdots,t_{n+m})$基本对称多项式. 这样就有

$$ S_I(\sigma_1'',\cdots,\sigma_k'')=\sum_{JK=I} S_J(\sigma)S_K(\sigma'). $$

左侧为$m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_{n+m})=\sum t_{a_1}^{i_1}\cdots t_{a_r}^{i_r},$ 令$J:=\{i_q:1\le a_q\le n\},$ $K:=\{i_q:n+1\le a_q\le n+m\},$ $JK=I.$

当$I$作为$k$划分就是$k=k,$ 记$S_k=S_I.$ 那么唯一的$JK=I=k$为$J=\varnothing$或$K=\varnothing.$

推论 7. $S_k(c(\omega\oplus\omega'))=S_k(c(\omega))+S_k(c(\omega')).$ $S_k$将和映到和.

注 8. 形式定义Chern特征$ch(\omega):=n+\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{S_k(c(\omega))}{k!}\in H^\pi(B;\mathbb{Q}),$ 那么$ch(\omega\oplus\omega')=ch(\omega)+ch(\omega'),$ $ch(\omega\otimes \omega')=ch(\omega)ch(\omega'),$ 形式上比Chern示性类要好.

$$ S_I[K^n]:=\left<{}S_I(c(\tau_K)),[K^n]\right>\in\mathbb{Z}. $$

推论 9. $S_I$的积公式: 对于$I$为$m+n$划分, $S_I[K^m\times L^n]=\sum_{I_1I_2=I}S_{I_1}[K^m]S_{I_2}[K^n].$

$\tau:=\tau_{K^m},$ $\tau':=\tau_{L^n},$ $\tau\times\tau'\cong (\pi_1^\ast \tau)\oplus(\pi_2^\ast \tau').$

$S_I[K\times L]=\left<{}S_I(\tau\times\tau'),[K\times L]\right>.$ 由前面的引理即有结果.

Pontrjagin number

For the real manifold $M^{4n},$ is a directional closed manifold, and $I=(i_1,\cdots,i_r)$ is the division of $n$. Define the $I$th Pontrjagin number as

$$ P_I[M^{4n}]=\left<{}p_{i_1}\cdots p_{i_r},[M^{4n}]\right>\in \mathbb{Z}. $$

Note 1. Note $\overline M$ as $M$ reverse orientation, $[\overline M]=-[M],$ but $p_k(\tau_M)=p_k(\tau_{\overline M}),$ and thus $P_I[\overline M]=-p_I[M].$

Corollary 2. If $M^{4n}$ Pontrjagin number is non-zero, then there is no counter-oriented diffeomorphism $M\rightarrow M.$

If $M=\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m,$ $p(\tau_{\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m})=(1+a^2)^{m+1},$ $a=-c_1(\gamma^1),$ $p_k(\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^m)=\binom{m+1}{k} a^{2k}\in H^{4k}.$ If $m=2n,$ Then $M^{4n}=\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n},$ $\,\forall\,I$ is the division of $n$, $p_{i_1}\cdots p_{i_r}[\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}]=\binom{2n+1}{i_1}\cdots \binom{2n+1}{i_r}\neq 0.$ As a corollary, there is no counter-oriented differential automorphism on $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}$.

For the case where $m$ is an odd number, the complex conjugate is an inversely oriented differential automorphism. Note that when $m$ is an odd number, There is no top Pontrjagin class.

Proposition 3. If $M^{4n}=\partial V^{4n+1},$ $V$ is compactly oriented, then all Pontrjagin numbers of $M^{4n}$ are zero.

Corollary 4. $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}\neq \partial V^{4n+1}$ (directional), and limited copy $\sqcup \mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n}\neq \partial V.$

Note that $\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^{2n+1}$ is not correct, There are examples$\mathbb{C}\mathrm{P}^1\approx S^2\approx \partial D^3.$

Theory of Symmetric Functions

Review $T^n=U(1)^{\times n}\hookrightarrow U(n),$ is reflected in the diagonal matrix. This induced $BT^n\rightarrow BU(n),$ Pull back to get $H^\ast (BT^n;\mathbb{Z})\leftarrow H^\ast (BU(n);\mathbb{Z}),$ $c_i\mapsto \sigma_i(t_1,\cdots t_n).$ $1+\sigma_1+\cdots+\sigma_n=(1+t_1)\cdots (1+t_n).$

Remember$S=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]=\oplus_{k=0}^\infty S^k,$ $S^k$ is a symmetric polynomial of degree $k$. Its basis is

$$ \{\sigma_{i_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_r}|i_1+\cdots+i_r=k,\quad i_j\le n\}. $$

$$ S^k=\mathbb{Z}\{\sigma_I|I\text{ 为$k$的划分, 每个元小于等于$n$}\}. $$

Two single terms are said to be equivalent if they differ by a substitution. For example, $t_1t_2^3t_3^5\sim t_4t_7^3t_2^5.$ Dividing $k$ into $I,$ $I$ a single symmetric polynomial is defined as

$$ m_I:=\sum \text{ 单项等价于 }t^I:=t_1^{i_1}\cdots t_r^{i_r}. $$

For example $k=3,$ $I=2+1=3,$ $m_I=t_1t_2^2+t_1^2t_2+t_1t_3^2+\cdots.$

Lemma 5. $\{m_I|\text{$I$为$k$划分, $r\le n$.}\}$ forms a set of $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of $S^k=S^k_{(n)}$.

This set of elements is obviously spread out into $S^k.$. Just check the dimension.

$$ \dim_\mathbb{Z}S^k_{(n)}=\#\{\sigma_I\}=\{I|i_j\le n\}. $$

I hope to prove that it is equal to $\{I|r\le n\}$. (Both are divided by $k$.)

We find that the former is Young diagrams with $k$ boxes below the $n$ column, The latter are Young diagrams with $k$ boxes below the $n$ line. For example, $I=(4,4,3,1,1),$ Arrange them in reverse order and place them like Tetris, then the number of columns is required not to exceed $n$. Its counterpart is $I'=(5,3,3,2),$, which is also arranged in reverse order and placed according to Tetris. It is required that the number of rows does not exceed $n.$. They are connected by transposition. Therefore, the number of sets is the same.

For any $k$ partition $I,$

$$ m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_n)\in \mathbb{Z}[t_1,\cdots,t_n]^{S_n}=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]. $$

$$ m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_n)=S_I(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n). $$

can be verified, $\,\forall\,n<k,$

$$ S_{I,n}(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n)=S_{I,k}(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n,0,\cdots,0); $$

$\,\forall\,n\ge k,$ $S_I$ remains unchanged.

Therefore, if $n\ge k,$ Then $\{m_I\}=\{S_I(\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n)\}$ constitutes a set of $\mathbb{Z}$-basis of $S^k=\mathbb{Z}[\sigma_1,\cdots,\sigma_n]$.

For example, $k=1,$ $S_1(\sigma_1)=\sigma_1,$

$$ S_2(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)=\sigma_1^2-2\sigma_2=(t_1+t_2)^2-2t_1t_2=t_1^2+t_2^2=m_2(t_1,t_2). $$

$$ S_{1,1}(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)=t_1t_2=\sigma_2, $$

$$ S _3=\sigma_1^3-3\sigma_1\sigma_2+3\sigma_3. $$

applied to topology,

$$ H^\ast (BU(n))\rightarrow H^\ast (BU(1)^{\times n})^{S_n},\quad c_i\mapsto \sigma_i(t_1,\cdots,t_n), $$

$\{c_I|I\text{ 为$k$的划分, }i_j\le n\}$ forms a set of $\mathbb{Z}$ bases of $H^{2k}(BU(n))$. After changing the base, $\{S_I(c_1,\cdots,c_n)|r\le n\}$ is also a set of $\mathbb{Z}$ bases.

For rank $n$ complex vector bundle $\omega,$ Definition$S_I(c(\omega)):=S_I(c_1(\omega),\cdots,c_n(\omega))\in H^{2n}(B;\mathbb{Z}).$

Lemma 6 (Thom). $S_I(c(\omega\oplus\omega'))=\sum_{JK=I}S_J(c(\omega))S_K(c(\omega')).$

Consider $\sigma_k$ to be $k$ basic symmetric polynomial ($t_1,\cdots,t_n$), $\sigma_k'$ is $(t_{n+1},\cdots,t_{n+m}),$ $\sigma_k''=\sum_{i=0}^k\sigma_i\sigma_{k-i}',$ Verify that $\sigma_k''$ is a basic symmetric polynomial of $(t_1,\cdots,t_{n+m})$. In this way, we have

$$ S_I(\sigma_1'',\cdots,\sigma_k'')=\sum_{JK=I} S_J(\sigma)S_K(\sigma'). $$

The left side is $m_I(t_1,\cdots,t_{n+m})=\sum t_{a_1}^{i_1}\cdots t_{a_r}^{i_r},$ Let $J:=\{i_q:1\le a_q\le n\},$ $K:=\{i_q:n+1\le a_q\le n+m\},$ $JK=I.$

When $I$ is divided as $k$, it is $k=k,$, recorded as $S_k=S_I.$ Then the only $JK=I=k$ is $J=\varnothing$ or $K=\varnothing.$

Corollary 7. $S_k(c(\omega\oplus\omega'))=S_k(c(\omega))+S_k(c(\omega')).$ $S_k$ Map sum to sum.

Note 8. The formal definition of Chern characteristics $ch(\omega):=n+\sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{S_k(c(\omega))}{k!}\in H^\pi(B;\mathbb{Q}),$ then $ch(\omega\oplus\omega')=ch(\omega)+ch(\omega'),$ $ch(\omega\otimes \omega')=ch(\omega)ch(\omega'),$ is formally better than the Chern characteristic class.

$$ S_I[K^n]:=\left<{}S_I(c(\tau_K)),[K^n]\right>\in\mathbb{Z}. $$

Corollary 9. Product formula of $S_I$: For $I$ divided by $m+n$, $S_I[K^m\times L^n]=\sum_{I_1I_2=I}S_{I_1}[K^m]S_{I_2}[K^n].$

$\tau:=\tau_{K^m},$ $\tau':=\tau_{L^n},$ $\tau\times\tau'\cong (\pi_1^\ast \tau)\oplus(\pi_2^\ast \tau').$

$S_I[K\times L]=\left<{}S_I(\tau\times\tau'),[K\times L]\right>.$ The result follows from the previous lemma.
