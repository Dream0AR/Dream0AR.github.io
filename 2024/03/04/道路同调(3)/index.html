有向图的连接

增广链复形

对于链复形

$$ 0\xleftarrow{\partial}\Lambda_0\xleftarrow{\partial}\Lambda_1\leftarrow\cdots, $$

我们考虑

$$ 0\xleftarrow{\partial} \mathbb{K}\xleftarrow{\partial}\Lambda_0\xleftarrow \Lambda_1\leftarrow\cdots,\quad \Lambda_{-1}:=\mathbb{K}, $$

其中$\partial e_i:=e,$ 为$\mathbb{K}$的单位元. 此时的同调群记为$\widetilde H_p,$ 称为约化同调群. 除了$\widetilde H_0=H_0/\mathbb{K},$ 模掉一个$\mathbb{K}$外, 其它同调群不变, 即$\widetilde H_p=H_p,$ $\,\forall\,p\ge 1,$ $\widetilde H_{-1}=\{0\}.$

记$\widetilde\beta_p=|\widetilde H_p|$为约化Betti数, 那么$\widetilde\beta_0=0$当且仅当$G$是连通的.

道路的连接

若$u=e_{i_0\cdots i_p},v=e_{j_0\cdots j_q}$分别是$X,Y$中的两条道路, 记其连接$u\ast v=e_{i_0\cdots i_p j_0\cdots j_q},$ 为$X\sqcup Y$中的道路. 做线性扩展, 我们有

$$ \ast :\Lambda_p(X)\times \Lambda_q(Y)\rightarrow \Lambda_{p+q+1}(X\sqcup Y). $$

引理 1. $\partial (u\ast v)=(\partial u)\ast v+(-1)^{p+1}u\ast \partial v,$ $u\in \Lambda_p(X).$

有向图的连接

有向图$Z:=X\ast Y$的点集由$X\sqcup Y$组成, 将所有$X$中的点连接到$Y$中的点, 即$x\rightarrow y,$ $\,\forall\,x\in X,$ $y\in Y.$ 这样就保证了$\mathcal{A}_p(X)\ast \mathcal{A}_q(Y)\subset \mathcal{A}_{p+q+1}(X\ast Y).$

引理 2. $\Omega_p(X)\ast \Omega_q(Y)\subset \Omega_{p+q+1}(X\ast Y),$ 对于约化同调群有同样的结论.

定理 3 (Künneth公式). $\,\forall\,p,q,r\ge -1,$ 我们有$\Omega_r(Z)= \bigoplus_{p+q+1=r}\Omega_p(X)\ast \Omega_q(Y).$ 进而$\widetilde H_r(Z)=\bigoplus_{p+q+1=r} \widetilde H_p(X)\ast \widetilde H_q(Y),$ $\widetilde\beta_r(Z)=\sum_{p+q+1=r} \widetilde\beta_p(X)\widetilde\beta_q(Y).$

借助这个公式, 我们可以得到对于有向图$X,$ 锥体$X\ast \{\bullet\}$是零调的, 双锥$X\ast \{\bullet,\bullet\}$的$r$-约化Betti数恰好是$X$的$r-1$-约化Betti数. 由此可以凭借$S^0=\{\bullet,\bullet\},$ 不断做双锥得到任意维球面$S^n,$ 满足$\widetilde\beta_n(S^n)=1$是唯一非平凡的约化Betti数.

Connection of directed graph

Augmented chain complex

For chain complex

we consider

Among them, $\partial ei:=e,$ is the unit element of $\mathbb{K}$.
The homology group at this time is recorded as $\widetilde H_p,$ which is called the reduced homology group.
Except $\widetilde H_0=H_0/\mathbb{K},$ modulates one $\mathbb{K}$,
Other homology groups remain unchanged, that is $\widetilde H_p=H_p,$ $\,\forall\,p\ge 1,$
$\widetilde H{-1}={0}.$

Let $\widetilde\beta_p=|\widetilde H_p|$ be the reduced Betti number,
Then $\widetilde\beta_0=0$ if and only if $G$ is connected.

Road connections

If $u=e{i_0\cdots i_p}, v=e{j0\cdots j_q}$ are two roads in $X, Y$ respectively,
Note that its connection $u\ast v=e{i_0\cdots i_p j_0\cdots j_q},$ is the road in $X\sqcup Y$.
Doing linear expansion, we have

Lemma 1. $\partial (u\ast v)=(\partial u)\ast v+(-1)^{p+1}u\ast \partial v,$ $u\in \Lambda_p(X).$

Connection of directed graph

The point set of the directed graph $Z:=X\ast Y$ is composed of $X\sqcup Y$, connecting all the points in $X$ to the points in $Y$,
That is $x\rightarrow y,$ $\,\forall\,x\in X,$ $y\in Y.$
This ensures $\mathcal{A}p(X)\ast \mathcal{A}_q(Y)\subset \mathcal{A}{p+q+1}(X\ast Y).$

Lemma 2. $\Omegap(X)\ast \Omega_q(Y)\subset \Omega{p+q+1}(X\ast Y),$ has the same conclusion for the reduced homology group.

Theorem 3 (Künneth formula). $\,\forall\,p,q,r\ge -1,$ We have $\Omegar(Z)= \bigoplus{p+q+1=r}\Omegap(X)\ast \Omega_q(Y).$ and then $\widetilde H_r(Z)=\bigoplus{p+q+1=r} \widetilde Hp(X)\ast \widetilde H_q(Y),$ $\widetilde\beta_r(Z)=\sum{p+q+1=r} \widetilde\beta_p(X)\widetilde\beta_q(Y).$

With the help of this formula, we can get that for the directed graph $X, $ the cone $X\ast {\bullet}$ is zero-modal,
The $r$-reduced Betti number of the bipyramid $X\ast {\bullet,\bullet}$ is exactly the $r-1$-reduced Betti number of $X$.
From this, we can rely on $S^0={\bullet,\bullet},$ to continuously make bicones to obtain any dimensional sphere $S^n,$
Satisfying $\widetilde\beta_n(S^n)=1$ is the only non-trivial reduced Betti number.
